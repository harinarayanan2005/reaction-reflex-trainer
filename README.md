## 🎮 8 Complete Game Modes & Training Arenas

### 1. ⚡ Visual Reflex (Human Benchmark)
- **Mechanics:** Click anywhere or hit `[SPACE]` the instant the red screen flashes neon **GREEN**.
- **Features:** Early click penalty warning, rolling 5-test average, and real-time millisecond accuracy.
- **Tiers:** Evaluates from *Godlike (<180ms)* down to *Casual (>265ms)*.

### 2. 🎯 Grid Shot (FPS Esports Warmup)
- **Mechanics:** 4x4 matrix grid with **3 targets active at all times**.
- **Flick Training:** Hitting a target immediately spawns a new one in an empty cell.
- **Metrics:** Live Pace (hits per second), total hits in 30 seconds, and flick latency. Includes spatial 3D audio panning!

### 3. 🔴 Target Blitz (Precision Aim)
- **Mechanics:** Randomly positioned shrinking neon targets.
- **Scoring:** +100 points per hit, -25 for misses. Test your micro-adjustments and aim speed.

### 4. 🎧 Audio Reaction (Auditory Stimulus)
- **Mechanics:** Pitch-black screen. Click immediately when you hear the procedural tone frequency.
- **Cognitive Comparison:** Compares auditory reaction vs visual reflexes (auditory is typically 30–50ms faster!).

### 5. ⌨️ Typing APM Velocity Rush
- **Mechanics:** Hacker command words descend from the top toward the glowing firewall line.
- **Action:** Type words into the cyber terminal bar and hit `[ENTER]` or `[SPACE]` to vaporize them before they breach.
- **Live Stats:** Real-time Words Per Minute (WPM), APM, and combo streak multipliers.

### 6. 🧠 Stroop Color Clash (Cognitive Interference)
- **Mechanics:** Word names appear in contrasting ink colors (e.g., the word "RED" displayed in Blue font).
- **Controls:** Click **MATCH** (or `[Left Arrow] / [A]`) or **MISMATCH** (`[Right Arrow] / [D]`).
- **Cognitive Training:** Trains the prefrontal cortex to overcome automatic reading impulses.

### 7. ⏱️ Tempo Precision (Rhythm Offset)
- **Mechanics:** 120 BPM rhythmic metronome with expanding visual pulse ring.
- **Feedback:** Hit `[SPACE]` on beat. Measures your exact offset error down to `+/- ms` with "PERFECT (<20ms)" ratings.

### 8. 🔢 Chimp Memory Sequence (Working Memory)
- **Mechanics:** Tiles 1 through N are scattered across a 20-cell grid.
- **The Twist:** Clicking `1` instantly turns all other tiles blank! You must recall where `2`, `3`, `4`, etc. were from spatial memory.

## 🎧 Procedural Multi-Pack Sound Synthesizer (`audio.js`)

ReflexForge Ultra contains a 100% self-contained Web Audio synthesizer with **4 selectable sound themes**:
- 🔊 **Cyber Synths:** Deep FM sweeps, punchy sine hits, and rising streak chords.
- 👾 **8-Bit Arcade:** Retro square waves, chiptune arpeggios, and classic video game dings.
- 🛸 **Sci-Fi Beams:** Sawtooth laser zaps, sub-bass drops, and alien telemetry tones.
- 🎵 **Clean Chimes:** Warm, minimal marimba-like acoustic frequencies.
- **Dynamic Spatial Audio:** Targets on the left side of the screen sound in the left ear; targets on the right sound in the right ear!
