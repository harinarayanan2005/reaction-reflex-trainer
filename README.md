# Reaction & Reflex Trainer

A simple, interactive web app to test and improve your reaction time, hand-eye coordination, and reflexes.

## Features

- **Visual Reaction**: Click or press space when the screen turns green.
- **Grid Shot**: Click targets on a grid to practice aiming speed.
- **Target Blitz**: Hit targets before they disappear.
- **Audio Reaction**: Test your reflexes using sound cues.
- **Typing Speed**: Type falling words before they hit the bottom.
- **Color Match**: Quick color-matching test.
- **Rhythm Precision**: Tap to the beat of a metronome.
- **Memory Sequence**: Remember and click numbers in the correct order.
- **Sound Effects**: Procedural audio feedback with multiple sound packs.
- **Stats & History**: View your past scores and reaction time charts.

## How to Run

No installation or build steps required. Simply open `index.html` in any web browser:

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Double-click `index.html` (or open it with your favorite browser).
